Dakbokkeumtang(Korean Chicken Stew)

Ingredient

  • Chicken (legs, thigh, breast)
  • oil
  • garlic
  • soy sauce
  • sugar
  • mirin
  • gochugaru
  • onion
  • black pepper
  • potatoe
  • oyster sauce
  • gochujang

Steps

  1. Put Oil
  2. Put Chicken
  3. add garlic
  4. Satue now
  5. Add 6 TBSP of soy sauce
  6. Put water
  7. 4 tbsp Sugar
  8. 3 tbsp of Mirin
  9. 4 tbsp of gochugaru
  10. 2 tbsp of oyster sauce
  11. 3 tbsp of gochujang
  12. put potatoe
  13. put onion and green onion
  14. put black pepper
